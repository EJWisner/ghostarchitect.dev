Ghost Architect™ Local 7.4.5 September 23, 2026 2 min read By Ernst J. Wisner

A merge or report line that never ends is cleaned up

The model writing a merge step or the final report sometimes starts a line and never finishes it, drifting into an endless run of loosely related words. Ghost now keeps such a line only up tluation now.

When a line runs past 40,000 characters, Ghost also stops the model there instead of letting it write to the end. On one scan this week that line reached 116,860 characters and took 22 minutes; stopped at 40,000, the same step takes about 9. A line that runs on for a while and then ends is left to finish, because the model often goes back to writing real findings after it.
