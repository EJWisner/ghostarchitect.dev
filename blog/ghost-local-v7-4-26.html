Ghost Architect™ Local 7.4.26 September 27, 2026 2 min read By Ernst J. Wisner

Monthly licenses that renew themselves

Ghost Architect™ Local is now priced by the month, a renewed license installs itself, and ghost --activate does what the license email says.

Solo is $80 a month, Small Team $400, Agency $1,000 and Enterprise $2,000, with half off the first month after an evaluation.

In the last three days before a monthly license expires, a connected install asks for its renewed license and installs it. Nothing is sent in zero-egress mode.

Air-gapped installs get the signed license file with every purchase and renewal email, and install it with ghost --install-license. A lapsed license now has 7 days of grace.

Full technical release notes for 7.4.26 are on the changelog, alongside every other Ghost Architect™ Local release.
