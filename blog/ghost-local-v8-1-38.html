Ghost Architect™ Local 8.1.38 November 17, 2026 2 min read By Ernst J. Wisner

Remediation velocity counts findings, from when they were first found

The Executive Weekly's remediation velocity now says what each number is a share of, and counts each finding once, timed from the day it was first found.

The weekly email used to print a share of findings resolved within 30 days just under the count of open findings, where it read as a share of the whole backlog. It now reads, for example: of the 173 findings now resolved, 168 (97 percent) were resolved within 30 days of first being found.

A finding that one scan missed and a later scan raised again used to count as fixed each time, usually within 30 days. Every figure in the section now counts each finding once, from the first time it was found, so a finding that was never fixed no longer reads as fixed quickly or as newly opened.

The REST API carries the counts beside the share. Its velocity figures now count findings the same way, so they change on the day you upgrade.
