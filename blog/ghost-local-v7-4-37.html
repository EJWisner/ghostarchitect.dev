Ghost Architect™ Local 7.4.37 September 30, 2026 2 min read By Ernst J. Wisner

A real finding keeps its rating, and a paused scan carries on

Two of Ghost Architect™ Local's checks set aside a finding that was real, and a scan paused by the scheduled backup could stay paused after an update. All three are fixed.

A finding that calls a catch silent or empty, where the catch logs the error, now stays in the report rated LOW at most (MEDIUM at most when the catch handles only a narrower kind of error), with the logging line named. A finding about a missing timeout is now checked in the file where the connection is made, not only the file that calls it.

Both changes were checked against findings we had verified by hand against the source they came from: no check now sets aside a finding that was real, or rates one below the severity it deserves.

A scan the scheduled backup had paused, when an update stood the machine's scans down at the same time, now carries on once the backup is done. A scan a person paused still waits for a person.
