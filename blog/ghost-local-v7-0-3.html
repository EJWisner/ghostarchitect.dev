Ghost Architect™ Local 7.0.3 August 29, 2026 2 min read By Ernst J. Wisner

Trust the portal's certificate, and reports that say what they could not do

The portal tells you how to trust its certificate: a guide, a warning before the first browser opens, and Admin Console prints the fingerprint and the trust commands for your platform.

A finding the report writer could not expand stays in the report as the analysis recorded it, and the writer runs on a measured budget instead of a fixed thirty seconds. The usage report no longer prints a token total it never measured. Underneath: the verifier no longer deletes a true finding because its own remediation pattern is present, a report the parser cannot read no longer reports a healthy codebase, a redaction marker corroborates a hardcoded-secret finding instead of refuting it, findings are written to the debug record as they enter verification so a scan can be replayed, and a retry that changes nothing is not retried. 438 of 438 smoke tests, 12 of 12 flows.
