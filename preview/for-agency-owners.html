// the problem

The margin is lost before the kickoff call.

It is almost never the team’s fault. The team executes. The problem is upstream: somebody priced a contract on a codebase nobody had read. By the time engineering opens the repo, the price is locked, the timeline is signed, and the only variable left is margin.

93%

of the 947 commercial codebases Black Duck audited for its 2026 report contained components with no development activity in two years.

92%

contained components four or more years out of date.

68%

had open source license conflicts, up from 56% a year earlier.

Source: Black Duck, 2026 Open Source Security and Risk Analysis.

Inherited code is where all of that hides, and a fixed bid is where it gets paid for. The senior engineer who would have caught it is on three other engagements. The codebase is someone else’s fifty thousand lines. The scoping call is Thursday and the proposal is due Friday. So the number goes out the door from the last engagement, rounded up.

Ghost Architect™ closes that gap. Same scoping engineer, starting from a finished read of the code instead of a blank repo. Repeatable. Auditable. On a flat monthly license, cheap enough to run on every prospect.

// honest answers

The questions you are actually thinking right now.

Does this replace my senior engineers?

No. It makes them more valuable. Ghost Architect™ surfaces the findings; your senior engineers decide what matters. The judgment, the architecture and the remediation stay human. It takes over the part of the job that should never have been senior work: reading fifty thousand lines of someone else’s code at one in the morning before a scoping call.

Is my client’s code uploaded to your servers?

No. We run no server that receives your client’s code. With Ghost Architect™ Local the analysis runs on your machines and nothing about the code leaves them while it works; zero-egress mode, included with every license, proves it with a printout. With Ghost Open™, the single-user CLI, code goes from your machine to Anthropic’s API on your own key, under Anthropic’s own data policy.

How does this compare to CodeRabbit, Kodus or other AI PR review tools?

Different moment, different buyer. AI PR review tools live inside your team’s Git workflow and comment on pull requests after the engagement has started. Ghost Architect™ runs before that: during due diligence, during scoping, during the pre-engagement audit, when you need to understand a codebase you do not own yet. Use both: one to win the bid and scope the work, the other to keep the engagement healthy once it is underway.

What does a scan cost to run?

With Ghost Architect™ Local, nothing per scan: one monthly license from $80 to $2,000, and a re-run costs machine time on hardware you already own. With Ghost Open™, you pay Anthropic for the tokens on your own key; the product prints the exact cost of every scan, and our public sample scan cost $0.2372.

My team will not use a new tool. Adoption will fail.

Most of the time the people who run it are you and one or two senior engineers, not the whole team, and that is fine. Point it at a folder, pick a scan, hand the report to whoever needs to read it. With Ghost Architect™ Local the firm shares one queue and one portal, and the Fleet page shows every scan, so nobody has to ask who ran what.

What about white-labeling? My firm’s brand matters.

Ghost Architect™ Local Agency and Enterprise include Ghost Partner™ profiles: your logo on the cover, your brand colors, your methodology in the executive summary, your billing rates in the cost estimates. The deliverable looks like your firm’s audit, because the judgment is yours. Ghost Open™ reports always carry Ghost Architect™ branding; white label is in Ghost Architect™ Local.

What if my engagement is not Magento?

Ghost Architect™ works on any codebase, any language, any platform: PHP, JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, Ruby, Java, Go, Rust, C# and the rest. Adobe Commerce and Magento come up often because that is where the founder spent nine years, but they are not a requirement. A React app, a Django backend, a Laravel monolith, a Node service, a Spring Boot app: same scans, same deliverable.

What is the catch?

Two honest ones. A local model is not a frontier model, so Ghost Architect™ checks findings against the source and tells you what it did not read rather than pretending otherwise. And you need a machine to run the model on. The trial is how you find out whether yours is enough, on your own code, before you pay.

Why hasn’t an established player built this already?

Because pre-engagement triage was not a software category until language models got good enough to read code. The large players are positioned on the other side of the engagement: their tools run after you already have the codebase. Ghost Architect™ runs before. We are founder-led and built for the moment the agency hits the scoping call.
