The first Local build: your model, your hardware, Ed25519 licences

The full architecture build.

A model-provider abstraction so the product runs on Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio, Jan.ai or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Ed25519 is the only licence signature scheme the product signs or verifies, and the key the product trusts is the public half of the key the activation service signs with. Recon reports carry an honest sizing notice instead of a fabricated 100/100 health score; a recon no longer writes a perfect score into a project's trend history or generates a client report for a scan that assessed nothing.
